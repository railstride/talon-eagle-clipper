# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy covers the Talon iPhone app and the Talon – Eagle Clipper plugin for Eagle.

## What we collect

Nothing. Neither the app nor the plugin has an account, analytics, advertising or tracking, and nothing you clip is sent to the developer.

## Where your clips go

1. When you share something to Talon, the app saves it to the folder you chose. That folder belongs to you. If it's in iCloud Drive or another cloud service, that service's own terms apply.
2. The plugin reads that folder on your Mac and adds the clips to your Eagle library.
3. Once Eagle confirms an import, the plugin deletes the clip from the folder.

## Network requests

When you share a web link, the app asks that website for the page title and preview image. This request goes directly to the website, like opening the page, and the website can see your IP address. Nothing else leaves your devices through Talon.

## Data stored on your devices

The app remembers which folder you chose. The plugin remembers the same, plus the IDs of recent imports so nothing is imported twice. Deleting the app or the plugin removes this.

## Children

Talon isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy appear here, with a new date. Questions go to [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/railstride/talon-eagle-clipper/issues).
